CHAPTER ONE · PREMATURE BURIAL

You weren’t
dead.

The last thing you remember is the familiar stillness. Another catalepsy episode. You couldn’t move. You couldn’t tell them you were still there.

Now you wake inside a coffin at the bottom of an open grave. Your grave. They buried you—but the earth hasn’t been filled in. Above you, the lid is splintered and broken open. You don’t remember breaking it.

You climb out into Blackthorn Cemetery. No mourners. No gravediggers. Only a bell in the distance, and shapes moving between the stones. You don’t know what happened here. You only know you need to leave.

You’ve woken from this before. Never somewhere like this.

Beside an abandoned shed, you find a wooden bat. You take it. Something beyond the graves has heard you.

Waves begin when you enter. Your ranks reset each run.
SURVIVOR’S JOURNAL

Getting the hang of it.

Food & rest

Each full day (12 active minutes) without eating or resting adds Hungry or Tired. Tired slows movement by 5%, 15%, 35%, then up to 75%. Hungry reduces stamina recovery by 3%, 9%, 21%, then up to 60%. Food and sleep arrive in chapter 2; this chapter has no food or rest actions. A new run resets both needs.

Lifetime record

Equipment

Q cycles carried weapons. E picks up nearby finds. Ammunition is finite. Search carefully: a few abandoned tools and rare weapons remain among the graves. Health boxes can occasionally be found on fallen enemies. Ranks, weapons and ammunition reset each run. A flashlight extends your visibility at night.
